Core Competencies

Small to very Large CNC Machining & Sheet Metal Parts & Assemblies

Precision Prototype and Production component part manufacturing from small to very large, from simple to very complex. We offer:

  • CNC 5 Axis Milling and Turning
  • CNC Horizontal & Vertical Milling up to 23 ft. x 138” x 59” on X, Y, & Z Axis with deep pocket milling capability. Supporting up to 55,000 lbs. on a rotary table with 59″ of travel.
  • CNC Vertical Turning up to 78-3/4” Ø & 63” tall
  • CNC Horizontal Turning to 30” Ø x 60” long
  • CNC Wire EDM & Sinker EDM
  • Manual Turning to 15” Ø x 20 ft. length
  • Sheet Metal Forming – 30 Ton CNC Turret Punch with 48” x 120” panel size, 110-Ton Press Brake and 8’ Shear.
