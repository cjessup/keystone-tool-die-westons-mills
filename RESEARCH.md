# Keystone Tool & Die, Inc. — research notes

Live site reviewed 2026-11-23: https://www.ktdny.com/ and every page linked from its menu.

## What the business is

Keystone Tool & Die, Inc. is a family-owned tool-and-die and custom manufacturing shop in Westons Mills, NY. The home page calls it a Quality Designer and Builder of Custom Manufacturing Equipment, Tooling, Fixtures and Gages, with a second on-site facility that formulates and casts Polyurethanes, Silicones, Epoxies and Urethane Foams. Customers named on the home page are Industrial and Military Customers and the Department of Defense. The menu also covers design and engineering, custom machinery, robotics and vision, program management, panel building, CNC and sheet metal, tooling, and quality.

## Peer sites

Layout ideas only. No text, photos, or branding were copied.

### Lone Star Tool Die & Metalworks — https://www.lstdm.com/

Family-style tool-and-die and custom manufacturing site. Borrowed: a short row of capability chips under the introduction, a tight capability list instead of large marketing cards, and a closing band that puts the phone next to a contact action.

### Schmid Tool and Engineering — https://www.schmidtool.com/

Tool-and-die shop that became a family-owned contract manufacturer. Borrowed: tell the founding and the work first, then an industries band, then the specific capacities as a plain list, with contact repeated rather than a price or a promise the source site does not make.

### Springfield Tool & Die — https://springfieldtoolanddie.com/

Engineered tooling shop. Borrowed: a numbered, photo-led index of what the shop actually builds (here, Keystone’s own competency photos and labels), and a page order of who they are, what they build, photographs, then how to call. Their statistics, markets, and process steps were not used.
